87% of drafted fields matched the public record on 20 rare diseases the tool had not seen (687 of 786). 0 fields stated anything missing from their cited source, and all 552 fields with no public answer (internal fields, or comparators where no drug is approved) were left blank. The other 13% were real records applied wrongly.

Most drug programs start with a Target Product Profile. About two-thirds of it is public. Teams usually assemble it by hand.

tppgen drafts that part with one command from FDA labels, trial registries and orphan designations, with a source on every field. What only a company knows, it leaves blank. It never guesses.
